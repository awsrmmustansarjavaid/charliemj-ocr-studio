## Ideas worth discussing
* **Table view** of the vocabulary with sorting and per-word status.
* **Image cleanup** before OCR (deskew, denoise, crop borders).
* **Highlight low-confidence words** in the result cards so you know what to double-check.
* **Per-language reading direction** (right-to-left editing for Urdu / Arabic / Persian).
* **Print / PDF export** from the editor.
* **Templates** for the editor (e.g. "flashcard sheet", "lesson notes").
